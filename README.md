# Breaking Bias, Building Bridges: Evaluation and Mitigation of Social Biases in LLMs via Contact Hypothesis

Chahat Raj, Anjishnu Mukherjee, Aylin Caliskan, Antonios Anastasopoulos, Ziwei Zhu. AIES 2024.

LLM answers follow the Contact Hypothesis from social psychology. A sentence of positive contact with a group lowers biased answers and negative contact raises them, and one epoch of instruction tuning LLaMA 2 on such prompts (Social Contact Debiasing) reduces biased responses by up to 40%.
